policies

payment

Prices are in US dollars. Graphics are paid in full before final, unwatermarked files are delivered. Websites are $50 (standard) or $70 (3D) for the build, paid at launch, then $25 a month for hosting, upkeep and updates.

revisions

Every piece includes two rounds of revisions. Extra rounds or big changes in direction after the design is approved may be quoted separately.

refunds & cancellations

Because every piece is made to order, payments are non-refundable once work has started. The website plan can be cancelled anytime; it stops at the end of the month already paid.

usage & credit

Once paid, you can use the final work for your game, socials and promotion. Archived Studio may show finished work in this portfolio unless you ask to keep it private. Previews are watermarked until payment.

privacy

Messages sent through the reply card go to the studio's email through FormSubmit and are only used to reply to you. This site doesn't use tracking cookies or ads, and your details are never sold or shared.
